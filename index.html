Hi, I am Jeetu.

Owner, Jeetu Book Store

I opened this shop a year ago because I love a good story and I felt our area needed a friendly place to find one. No big mall, no fancy rules. Just walk in, tell me what you like, and I will hand you something good.

Some people want a funny book to relax, some want a horror tale to scare their friends, and some want a family story to read with parents. I keep all of them on my shelves and I enjoy matching each person to the right one.

It has been one year, and the best part is seeing the same faces come back for their next book.
